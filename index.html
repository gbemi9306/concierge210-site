    Call, text, or email. Tell me what's going on — a parent you're worried about, or a move you're trying to get ahead of.

    We talk through the situation and figure out which package actually fits. No pressure, no sales pitch.

Questions families ask

Is this medical care?

No. I'm not a nurse or a medical provider. I coordinate, check in, and handle logistics — for anything medical, I work alongside your parent's existing doctors and caregivers, not in place of them.

What areas do you serve?

The San Antonio metro area, including JBSA-Lackland, JBSA-Fort Sam Houston, and JBSA-Randolph.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Zelle, or Cash App. Check-in service is billed monthly up front; move-in packages are a flat fee paid before I start.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fee — just let me know before the next billing date.

What if something urgent comes up?

Premium check-in clients get a same-week response from me for anything that feels off. For a true emergency, call 911 first — I'll coordinate with family after.
